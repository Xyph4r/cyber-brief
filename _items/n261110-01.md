- **France blames Iran for the supermarket refrigeration attacks** `#threat` `#ot` `UPDATE` — French authorities attribute the October attacks, which disrupted cooling at 700+ supermarkets and retail sites, to Shahid Kaveh, an IRGC cyber unit previously tied to attacks on US water-utility controllers. The attacks caused food losses and at least 15 fires. *Why it matters:* this is the first formal attribution and it confirms state-backed sabotage with physical consequences.
