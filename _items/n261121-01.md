- **European Commission confirms AI-assisted cyberattack disrupted services** `#breach` `#ai` — The attack, detected on 22 October, took the Sysper HR platform, the MyPMO medical-reimbursement system and translation systems offline as a precaution; most are restored and no staff data breach has been found. It has not been attributed, and the AI tool has not been named, though CERT-EU recently warned about the AI pentest framework Artex. *Why it matters:* it is an EU institution publicly acknowledging AI-assisted offensive tooling used against it.
