- **CVE-2026-19868 — SonicWall SSL-VPN SAML/MFA bypass** `#vuln` `HIGH` `EXPLOITED` `KEV` `PATCH` — Attackers holding valid credentials for an alternate auth method can bypass SAML controls, including SAML-enforced MFA, on SonicOS Gen7 (≤7.3.3-7015) and Gen8 (≤8.2.2-8015). Fixed in 7.3.4-7013 and 8.2.3-8015; no workaround. *Why it matters:* MFA on the VPN edge is not a guarantee, and CISA has confirmed exploitation.
