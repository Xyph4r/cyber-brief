- **SilentAir: new state-level actor intercepts calls and SMS from inside mobile networks** `#threat` — Enea reports that SilentAir hijacks operator network nodes and uses SS7 (sometimes Diameter) signaling to reroute calls and SMS, and sends silent SMS to confirm interception. Operators in at least six Asian countries are affected, Taiwan most, and political and government figures elsewhere are targeted. Low-confidence China-nexus link; the report also covers Liminal Panda and its GTPDoor backdoor on IPX networks.
