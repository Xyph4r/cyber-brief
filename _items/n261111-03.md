- **Qubole supply-chain attack: a dozen-plus organizations breached through AWS cross-account roles** `#breach` `#supply-chain` — An attacker used compromised IAM credentials of Qubole's AWS account to assume roles customers had created, many left behind after they stopped using the platform, then harvested secrets and exfiltrated whole S3 buckets (14–26 October). *Why it matters:* it is a clean example of stale third-party trust relationships; audit and remove unused cross-account roles.
