- **N-able "4EverAble2Shell" zero-day exploited hours after PoC** `#vuln` `ZERO-DAY` `EXPLOITED` `UPDATE` — Chains an Apache Axis flaw (CVE-2026-90436) with an N-central privilege escalation for root command execution, requiring the Legacy API to be enabled. Exploitation began 12 November; at least 21 servers are compromised, mostly running Monero miners, 19 of them on the 10 November Hotfix 2 build. N-able disabled the Intune integration and urges on-prem customers to confirm the Legacy API is off. *Why it matters:* the hotfixes covered yesterday did not close this; RMM platforms remain a prime MSP supply-chain target.
