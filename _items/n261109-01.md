- **Iris Ohyama: third-party cloud breach may expose 7.7 million customers** `#breach` — The Japanese manufacturer says unauthorized access to a third-party cloud service may have exposed names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, birth dates and (encrypted, for active members) passwords of about 7.7M people. Card data was not stored there. *Why it matters:* it adds to the wave of Japanese incidents (see the emergency package covered Saturday) and again comes through a third-party provider.
