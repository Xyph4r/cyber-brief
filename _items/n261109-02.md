- **APT29 mobile espionage campaign abuses already-patched iOS and Android flaws** `#threat` — Sekoia attributes a campaign to Russian state-linked APT29. It used the DarkSword toolkit against iOS 18.5, cookie theft on old iOS 16, and outdated Chrome on Android, delivered through compromised sites, spoofed sites and a hacked Ukrainian analytics script. *Why it matters:* fully updated devices should be safe, which makes this a patching-discipline story.
