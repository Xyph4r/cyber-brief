- **AI finds 66 new flaws in TCP/IP stacks of unpatchable devices** `#vuln` `#ai` — Forescout's Claude-based pipeline confirmed 99 vulnerabilities across 10 TCP/IP stacks, 66 of them previously unknown, in about 10 hours of automated work plus roughly 80 hours of validation, versus nine months for the earlier manual project. *Why it matters:* AI sharply cuts the cost of vulnerability research, and some affected vendors no longer exist, so containment (segmentation) matters more than patching.
