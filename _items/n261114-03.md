- **Citrix fixes 10 critical NetScaler flaws** `#vuln` `CRITICAL` `PATCH` — Nine critical memory-overflow bugs in NetScaler ADC/Gateway (e.g. CVE-2026-120868, CVE-2026-107407) mostly give DoS or unpredictable behavior, and CVE-2026-120869 (CVSS 9.4) in NetScaler Console allows an unauthenticated control bypass. Fixed in ADC/Gateway 14.1-73.57, 13.1-64.39 and Console 14.1-74.34. No exploitation reported. *Why it matters:* NetScaler is a frequent attack target, so patch quickly. [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/citrix-patches-10-critical-netscaler-vulnerabilities/)
