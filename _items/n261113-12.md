- **SCKit: worm-like toolkit targets MSPs in ScreenConnect attacks** `#threat` `UPDATE` — Huntress and Blackpoint describe 16 scripts and components that keep ScreenConnect access on attacker servers; the RelayTrap component tries to exploit CVE-2026-84869 against technician workstations and others steal auth cookies to push commands via Shared Toolbox. Self-spreading has not been seen to succeed; Huntress thinks RelayTrap was LLM-generated. Initial access is linked to RMM+ 1.0.31/1.0.32. Follows yesterday's ScreenConnect MSP campaign.
