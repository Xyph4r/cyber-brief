- **FastUnlock: unpatched Windows zero-day runs programs from the lock screen** `#vuln` `ZERO-DAY` `MEDIUM` — Researcher "Nightmare Eclipse" disclosed a technique that abuses the Narrator screen reader to launch programs on a locked PC, in a non-admin context; it needs physical access and a reachable Microsoft account, and ThreatLocker confirmed it works. There is no Microsoft fix yet. Mitigations: add a BitLocker startup PIN, shut down or hibernate devices that leave your control, and don't rely on the lock screen alone. *Why it matters:* it is another physical-access bypass of the lock screen, relevant to lost or stolen laptops.
