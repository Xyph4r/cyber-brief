- **VMware vCenter auth bypass exploited as a zero-day by suspected Chinese APT** `#vuln` `CRITICAL` `ZERO-DAY` `EXPLOITED` `PATCH` — CVE-2026-123890 (CVSS 9.8) in the VMware Directory Service gives an unauthenticated attacker an admin session. Mandiant ties it to Armory Castle (formerly UNC5221 / Warp Panda), which got in via a BeyondTrust Remote Support zero-day, stole SAML signing material, forged tokens and moved on to ESXi. Fixed in vCenter 9.1.1.0200, 9.0.2.0200 and 8.0 U3m; vCenter 7.0 is out of support with no public patch. *Why it matters:* vCenter controls the whole virtual estate, and the attackers held SSO and encryption keys, so patching alone does not evict them.
