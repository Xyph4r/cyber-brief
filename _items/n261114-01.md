- **FAA orders nationwide ground stop over "extraordinary circumstances"** `#incident` — On 13 November the FAA halted departures at all US airports after a communications problem affecting air route traffic control centers; Chicago had already been stopped. Officials had not said what caused it, and it is unclear whether a cyberattack is involved. *Why it matters:* a critical-infrastructure outage of this scale will prompt immediate questions about cause, so watch for the root-cause statement.
