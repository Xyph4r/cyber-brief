- **PHP2Shell exploited in destructive attacks on Check Point gateways** `#vuln` `#threat` `CRITICAL` `EXPLOITED` `PATCH` — Attackers chain PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618 / CVE-2026-109619, patched 21 October) with the local root escalation CVE-2026-123039 in Security Gateway and Mobile Access appliances, then plant webshells and PhiliKit-variant backdoors (TSGA, TSProxy). A first wave from 30 October hit Israeli organizations and, in one case, moved to an ESXi host and ran a wiper; a second wave from 2 November hit several countries without destructive activity. Check Point could not attribute it; the tooling is China-linked but the wiper does not match known Chinese operations. *Why it matters:* edge appliances are exploited within days of patches, and a wiper turns it into more than espionage. [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/php2shell-exploited-in-destructive-check-point-gateway-attacks/)
