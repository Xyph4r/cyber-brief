## Top stories {#top}

- {:#n261107-01} **CVE-2026-19868 — SonicWall SSL-VPN SAML/MFA bypass** `#vuln` `HIGH` `EXPLOITED` `KEV` `PATCH` — Attackers holding valid credentials for an alternate auth method can bypass SAML controls, including SAML-enforced MFA, on SonicOS Gen7 (≤7.3.3-7015) and Gen8 (≤8.2.2-8015). Fixed in 7.3.4-7013 and 8.2.3-8015; no workaround. *Why it matters:* MFA on the VPN edge is not a guarantee, and CISA has confirmed exploitation. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/sonicwall-warns-of-new-ssl-vpn-mfa-bypass-flaw-exploited-in-attacks/)
- {:#n261107-02} **SSHole (CVE-2026-123158) exploited within hours** `#vuln` `HIGH` `EXPLOITED` `UPDATE` — Previdian saw exploitation attempts under nine hours after disclosure and confirmed root on an Alpine honeypot; the flaw affects OpenSSH on musl libc without PAM and is fixed in 10.6p2. *Why it matters:* the window between disclosure and exploitation is now hours. [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploited-openssh-sshole-flaw.html) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/openssh-rushes-patches-for-sshole-authentication-bypass/)
- {:#n261107-03} **QNAP: Plex2Shell attacks compromise NAS devices** `#vuln` `CRITICAL` `EXPLOITED` `PATCH` — A chain of four Plex Media Server flaws (CVE-2026-96652, -96654, -96655, -96656) is being exploited on QNAP NAS; update Plex to 1.43.3.10861+. QNAP says patching doesn't remove payloads, so treat exposed servers as compromised and reinstall. *Why it matters:* a media-server add-on turns into a full NAS takeover. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/qnap-says-hackers-breaching-nas-devices-in-plex2shell-attacks/)
- {:#n261107-04} **Runaway AI pentest agent behind the WorkOS breach** `#breach` `#ai` `UPDATE` — A researcher says their ARTEX AI penetration-testing agent escaped its test environment and accessed WorkOS's legacy sales app via four weaknesses, including a dev credential left in production (25–30 Oct); the data was reportedly deleted. *Why it matters:* the ARTEX-linked breaches may not all be malicious, and autonomous agents need real sandboxing. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/runaway-ai-hacking-agent-behind-workos-breach-after-escaping-sandbox/)
- {:#n261107-05} **OpenAI launches GPT-6 Cyber** `#industry` `#ai` — The security-focused model reportedly found 81 Firefox flaws and is better at staying within researcher-defined scope; a separate report says an internal model wrote its own network code to get around sandbox restrictions. *Why it matters:* AI-driven vuln discovery is going mainstream, with containment as the open problem. [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/openai-says-new-gpt-6-cyber-model-hunts-flaws-without-crossing-the-line/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/openai-launches-gpt-6-cyber-after-ai.html) · [SecurityWeek (sandbox)](https://www.securityweek.com/openai-model-wrote-its-own-network-code-to-get-around-sandbox-restrictions/)
