## Top stories {#top}

- **France blames Iran for the supermarket refrigeration attacks** `#threat` `#ot` `UPDATE` — French authorities attribute the October attacks, which disrupted cooling at 700+ supermarkets and retail sites, to Shahid Kaveh, an IRGC cyber unit previously tied to attacks on US water-utility controllers. The attacks caused food losses and at least 15 fires. *Why it matters:* this is the first formal attribution and it confirms state-backed sabotage with physical consequences. [The Record](https://therecord.media/france-blames-iran-for-fridge-cyberattacks) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/france-blames-iranian-hackers-for-supermarket-attacks-that-sparked-fires/)
- **N-able takes cloud N-central offline after 30+ alleged zero-days published** `#vuln` `ZERO-DAY` — A researcher publicly disclosed more than 30 alleged zero-days, including exploits that could allow takeover. N-able took its hosted N-central environments offline and told on-premises customers to shut their servers down. *Why it matters:* RMM tools sit with admin rights across many customers, so this is a potential MSP supply-chain risk. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/n-able-takes-cloud-platform-offline-after-30-plus-zero-days-published/)
- **AI-assisted attack breaches German web host Mittwald via its AI-assistant interface** `#breach` `#ai` `#germany` — The company disclosed that attackers, allegedly aided by AI, got in through a tool that lets customers connect AI assistants to their accounts. *Why it matters:* AI-agent integration points are a new attack surface for hosting and cloud providers. [The Record](https://therecord.media/cyberattack-germany-ai-mittwald)
- **SSHole exploitation now confirmed in the wild on Linux systems** `#vuln` `HIGH` `EXPLOITED` `UPDATE` — Both BleepingComputer and SecurityWeek report active attacks on OpenSSH (musl libc, no PAM); the fix is in 10.6p2. *Why it matters:* it moves from honeypot proof-of-concept (covered Saturday) to real compromises. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-exploit-sshole-openssh-flaw-to-breach-linux-systems/) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/hackers-exploiting-sshole-authentication-bypass-in-openssh/)
