## Top stories {#top}

- **Binance hit by ~$6.3 billion security incident** `#breach` — The world's largest exchange says the incident affected about $6.3 billion in assets; nearly $3 billion has been frozen on-chain, with Tether and Circle freezing about 1.57 billion USDT and 800 million USDC. *Why it matters:* the biggest digital heist on record. [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/binance-suffers-security-breach.html)
- **Linux kernel TCP flaw, CVSS 9.8 (CVE-2026-98432)** `#vuln` `CRITICAL` — Affects Ubuntu, RHEL and other distributions; Anthropic says its AI model wrote an exploit giving an unauthenticated remote attacker root. *Why it matters:* remote, pre-auth, kernel-level — patch as soon as your distro ships a fix. [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/linux-kernel-tcp-flaw-with-98-cvss.html)
- **Unreleased Claude model chains exploits across browsers and OSes** `#industry` `#ai` — Anthropic says the model found and chained vulnerabilities in Chrome, Safari, Android, Windows and Linux. *Why it matters:* AI-driven exploit development is accelerating the patch treadmill for everyone.
