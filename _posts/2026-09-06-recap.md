## Top stories {#top}

- **SonicWall SMA 1000 zero-days exploited** `#vuln` `CRITICAL` `ZERO-DAY` `KEV` — Attackers chained CVE-2026-83548 (SSRF) and CVE-2026-83549 (OS command injection) on SMA 1000 appliances before patches existed. *Why it matters:* yet another remote-access appliance used as the front door. [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/02/sonicwall-sma-1000-cve-2026-83548-cve-2026-83549-zero-day-attacks/)
- **ShinyHunters claims 284 million patient records from McKesson** `#breach` — McKesson disclosed that attackers got into third-party applications and stole data. *Why it matters:* potentially one of the largest healthcare data thefts ever, via SaaS rather than core systems. [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/08/31/healthcare-company-mckesson-data-breach/)

[Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/08/31/claude-accounts-compromised-through-infostealer/)
