## Top stories {#top}

- **FBI and CISA warn of attacks on refrigeration and cold-chain systems** `#threat` `#ot` `UPDATE` — The agencies say attackers are targeting industrial refrigeration and cold-chain monitoring systems, which can spoil food and damage equipment. This follows the supermarket and French blood-storage incidents covered earlier this week. *Why it matters:* it is the first official US guidance on a campaign that is causing physical damage. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fbi-cisa-warn-of-cyberattacks-targeting-refrigeration-systems/) · [SANS ISC Stormcast](https://isc.sans.edu/podcastdetail/10168)
- **Patched Zyxel GS1900 switches still exploitable (CVE-2026-107441)** `#vuln` `HIGH` `ZERO-DAY` `EXPLOITED` `UPDATE` — Switches that already had the earlier fix remain open to unauthenticated command execution through crafted HTTP requests, so new firmware is required. *Why it matters:* applying the first patch is not enough, so check the firmware version you are actually running. [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/05/zyxel-cve-2026-107441-exploit/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/zyxel-gs1900-patch-fails-to-block-zero.html) · [heise](https://www.heise.de/news/Jetzt-patchen-Attacken-auf-Zyxel-Switches-beobachtet-11512953.html)
