## Top stories {#top}

- {:#n261109-01} **Iris Ohyama: third-party cloud breach may expose 7.7 million customers** `#breach` — The Japanese manufacturer says unauthorized access to a third-party cloud service may have exposed names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, birth dates and (encrypted, for active members) passwords of about 7.7M people. Card data was not stored there. *Why it matters:* it adds to the wave of Japanese incidents (see the emergency package covered Saturday) and again comes through a third-party provider. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/iris-ohyama-data-cloud-breach-exposes-info-of-77-million-customers/)
- {:#n261109-02} **APT29 mobile espionage campaign abuses already-patched iOS and Android flaws** `#threat` — Sekoia attributes a campaign to Russian state-linked APT29. It used the DarkSword toolkit against iOS 18.5, cookie theft on old iOS 16, and outdated Chrome on Android, delivered through compromised sites, spoofed sites and a hacked Ukrainian analytics script. *Why it matters:* fully updated devices should be safe, which makes this a patching-discipline story. [The Record](https://therecord.media/russia-hackers-smartphone-cyberattacks)
- {:#n261109-03} **Patch Tuesday is tomorrow (10 November)** `#vuln` `PATCH` — Expect fixes across Windows and Office; Help Net Security's forecast anticipates a lot of legacy software. Meanwhile, the SonicWall SAML MFA bypass (CVE-2026-19868, KEV), the OpenSSH SSHole flaw (fixed in 10.6p2) and the QNAP/Plex2Shell chain from last week remain priorities to patch. [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/06/november-2026-patch-tuesday-forecast/) · [SecurityWeek (SonicWall)](https://www.securityweek.com/sonicwall-patches-exploited-sonicos-bug-that-weakens-vpn-logins/)
