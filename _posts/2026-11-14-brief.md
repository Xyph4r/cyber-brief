## Top stories {#top}

- {:#n261114-01} **FAA orders nationwide ground stop over "extraordinary circumstances"** `#incident` — On 13 November the FAA halted departures at all US airports after a communications problem affecting air route traffic control centers; Chicago had already been stopped. Officials had not said what caused it, and it is unclear whether a cyberattack is involved. *Why it matters:* a critical-infrastructure outage of this scale will prompt immediate questions about cause, so watch for the root-cause statement. [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/faa-ground-stop-issued-due-to-extraordinary-circumstances/)
- {:#n261114-02} **PHP2Shell exploited in destructive attacks on Check Point gateways** `#vuln` `#threat` `CRITICAL` `EXPLOITED` `PATCH` — Attackers chain PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618 / CVE-2026-109619, patched 21 October) with the local root escalation CVE-2026-123039 in Security Gateway and Mobile Access appliances, then plant webshells and PhiliKit-variant backdoors (TSGA, TSProxy). A first wave from 30 October hit Israeli organizations and, in one case, moved to an ESXi host and ran a wiper; a second wave from 2 November hit several countries without destructive activity. Check Point could not attribute it; the tooling is China-linked but the wiper does not match known Chinese operations. *Why it matters:* edge appliances are exploited within days of patches, and a wiper turns it into more than espionage. [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/php2shell-exploited-in-destructive-check-point-gateway-attacks/)
- {:#n261114-03} **Citrix fixes 10 critical NetScaler flaws** `#vuln` `CRITICAL` `PATCH` — Nine critical memory-overflow bugs in NetScaler ADC/Gateway (e.g. CVE-2026-120868, CVE-2026-107407) mostly give DoS or unpredictable behavior, and CVE-2026-120869 (CVSS 9.4) in NetScaler Console allows an unauthenticated control bypass. Fixed in ADC/Gateway 14.1-73.57, 13.1-64.39 and Console 14.1-74.34. No exploitation reported. *Why it matters:* NetScaler is a frequent attack target, so patch quickly. [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/citrix-patches-10-critical-netscaler-vulnerabilities/)

## Threat intel & red team {#threat-intel}

- {:#n261114-04} **SilentAir: new state-level actor intercepts calls and SMS from inside mobile networks** `#threat` — Enea reports that SilentAir hijacks operator network nodes and uses SS7 (sometimes Diameter) signaling to reroute calls and SMS, and sends silent SMS to confirm interception. Operators in at least six Asian countries are affected, Taiwan most, and political and government figures elsewhere are targeted. Low-confidence China-nexus link; the report also covers Liminal Panda and its GTPDoor backdoor on IPX networks. [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/silentair-hackers-intercept-calls-sms-from-inside-mobile-networks/)
