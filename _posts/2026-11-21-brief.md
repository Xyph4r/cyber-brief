## Top stories {#top}

- {:#n261121-01} **European Commission confirms AI-assisted cyberattack disrupted services** `#breach` `#ai` — The attack, detected on 22 October, took the Sysper HR platform, the MyPMO medical-reimbursement system and translation systems offline as a precaution; most are restored and no staff data breach has been found. It has not been attributed, and the AI tool has not been named, though CERT-EU recently warned about the AI pentest framework Artex. *Why it matters:* it is an EU institution publicly acknowledging AI-assisted offensive tooling used against it. [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/european-commission-confirms-ai-assisted-cyberattack-disrupted-services/)
- {:#n261121-02} **FastUnlock: unpatched Windows zero-day runs programs from the lock screen** `#vuln` `ZERO-DAY` `MEDIUM` — Researcher "Nightmare Eclipse" disclosed a technique that abuses the Narrator screen reader to launch programs on a locked PC, in a non-admin context; it needs physical access and a reachable Microsoft account, and ThreatLocker confirmed it works. There is no Microsoft fix yet. Mitigations: add a BitLocker startup PIN, shut down or hibernate devices that leave your control, and don't rely on the lock screen alone. *Why it matters:* it is another physical-access bypass of the lock screen, relevant to lost or stolen laptops. [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/new-unpatched-windows-0-day-fastunlock.html) · [SecurityWeek roundup](https://www.securityweek.com/in-other-news-norwegian-energy-firm-cyberattacks-supercomputer-hack-new-nightmare-eclipse-exploit/)

[SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/jpmorgan-visa-mastercard-cite-third-party-incidents-amid-data-leak/)
