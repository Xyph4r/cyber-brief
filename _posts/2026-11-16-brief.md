## Top stories {#top}

- {:#n261116-01} **AI finds 66 new flaws in TCP/IP stacks of unpatchable devices** `#vuln` `#ai` — Forescout's Claude-based pipeline confirmed 99 vulnerabilities across 10 TCP/IP stacks, 66 of them previously unknown, in about 10 hours of automated work plus roughly 80 hours of validation, versus nine months for the earlier manual project. *Why it matters:* AI sharply cuts the cost of vulnerability research, and some affected vendors no longer exist, so containment (segmentation) matters more than patching. [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/16/forescout-ai-models-vulnerability-research/)
- {:#n261116-02} **Italy fines Meta €805M over Facebook's Android gallery access** `#industry` `#eu` — Italy's privacy regulator penalised Meta over a Facebook Android feature that suggested photos from the phone gallery without explicit consent; Meta plans to appeal. *Why it matters:* a large GDPR-style enforcement action on consent for on-device data. [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/italy-fines-meta-805-million-over.html) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/meta-fined-887-million-in-italy-over-android-gallery-photos-and-videos-on-facebook/)
- {:#n261116-03} **UnrealIRCd flaw exploited, added to CISA KEV** `#vuln` `CRITICAL` `EXPLOITED` `KEV` — CVE-2026-114713 allows command injection that runs with the server's privileges and is now under active exploitation. *Why it matters:* patch or isolate any internet-facing IRC daemon; KEV listing sets a federal deadline.
